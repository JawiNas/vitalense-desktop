VitaLense privacy policy

Effective 13 November 2026

In short: VitaLense keeps everything you enter on your own device. We do not run accounts, servers, analytics, advertising or tracking, and we do not sell or share your data.

Who we are

VitaLense is published by Infrability, powered by AI Vortex (“we”, “us”).

Information you enter

To give you results and a plan, VitaLense asks for details such as your name (optional), sex, age, height, weight, activity level, body measurements, lifestyle answers for the bio-age estimate, food preferences, allergies, training preferences, water intake, fasting times, and the workouts you plan and log (exercises, sets, weights, repetitions and personal records).

This information is stored only in the app’s local storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. If you use your phone’s own backup (such as iCloud or Google backup), the app’s data may be included in that encrypted backup under your account.

Health information

Some of what you enter relates to your health and fitness. It stays on your device, is used only to calculate your results and plans inside the app, and is never used for advertising or shared with third parties.

Progress photos

If you add progress photos, they are stored only in this device’s own app storage. They are made smaller and re-saved when you add them, which removes location and camera details. They are not part of the backup file and are never uploaded. Deleting a photo, or using Settings → Delete all my data, removes them.

Recipe photos

Recipe and drink photos are loaded from Unsplash’s image servers (images.unsplash.com) when you view them. Like any website, Unsplash receives standard technical information with each image request, such as your IP address and browser type. No information you enter in VitaLense is sent with these requests. Photos are then kept in your device’s cache so they can show offline. Unsplash’s own privacy policy is at unsplash.com/privacy.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders or rest-timer alerts, the app schedules local notifications on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

Desktop app updates

The Windows and Mac apps check for new versions on GitHub, where the installers are published. GitHub receives standard technical information with that request, such as your IP address. No information you enter in VitaLense is sent.

Third parties

VitaLense contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software. The website version may be served by a hosting provider (such as Vercel or GitHub Pages), which can keep standard server logs like IP addresses for security; VitaLense itself does not collect them.

Sharing and backups

Data only leaves your device when you choose to share a result, share or print a training plan, or export a backup or spreadsheet, and it goes only where you send it. Importing a file from another workout app reads it on your device only.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → Delete all my data.
  • Or uninstall the app (or clear the website’s data in your browser).

Children

VitaLense is designed for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Medical disclaimer

VitaLense provides general wellness information. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Talk to a qualified health professional before changing your diet or exercise routine.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
